Method 1 (Instant) Run Immediately via Wi-Fi / PWA
Takes 15 seconds

The BabyHealth Guard server is running live on this computer. You can open and install it directly on your Android phone as an app:

  1. 1 Make sure your Android phone is connected to the same Wi-Fi network as this laptop (or connect phone to laptop hotspot).
  2. 2
    Open Chrome on your Android phone and go to:
    http://192.168.1.135:8080/mobile/dashboard_preview.html
  3. 3 In Chrome on your phone, tap the 3 dots (⋮) in the top-right corner → tap "Add to Home screen" (or "Install app"). It will place an icon on your phone and open in full-screen mode like a downloaded app!
Scan with your Phone Camera

Open your phone's camera or Google Lens and point it at this QR code to launch the Mother Dashboard instantly.

Method 2 (Native App) Build & Install APK with Android Studio
Full Offline Binary

The project includes a ready-to-run Flutter mobile project in mobile/:

  1. 1 Open Android Studio → Click Open → Select folder:
    C:\Users\Admin\BabyHealth-Guard\mobile
  2. 2 On your Android phone, enable Developer Options (go to Settings → About Phone → tap Build Number 7 times), then go to Settings → System → Developer options → turn on USB Debugging.
  3. 3 Connect your phone to your PC with a USB cable. When prompted on the phone, tap Allow USB debugging.
  4. 4 In Android Studio, select your phone in the device dropdown at the top and click the green Run (▶) button (or press Shift + F10).
  5. 5 To generate a standalone APK file to share via WhatsApp or Bluetooth, run in terminal:
    flutter build apk --release
