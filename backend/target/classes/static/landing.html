Free 2G USSD Gateway via *920#

Mothers and village health teams without smartphones or internet connection can dial *920# from any basic mobile phone across MTN, Airtel, and Uganda Telecom networks.

1 Dial *920# to open the BabyHealth Guard Care Menu.
2 Select 1. Register Pregnancy or 2. Log Daily Check-In.
3 Select 3. Next ANC Visit to receive date and clinic room details.
4 Select 4. Emergency Alert to dispatch village health team midwives.
